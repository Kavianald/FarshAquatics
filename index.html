About Dina Farshchi

Dina is a certified lifeguard and experienced swim instructor with a background in private-club instruction and shift leadership.

Since 2019, Dina has coached swimmers across ages and abilities—emphasizing clear communication, technique, and confidence in and around the water. She has served as a Swim Instructor & Shift Supervisor at a private club, coordinating teams and maintaining high service standards.

Lessons and lifeguarding are available across Vancouver, West Vancouver, and North Vancouver — at your home, condo, or club pool, or at a partner facility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does Farsh Aquatics offer swim lessons?

Farsh Aquatics serves Vancouver and the North Shore, including West Vancouver and North Vancouver. Sessions can take place at your home, condo, or club pool, or at a partner facility — whichever works best for you.

What ages and skill levels do you teach?

All ages and levels — from toddlers and kids building water confidence to adults refining stroke technique or training for endurance.

Can I hire a private lifeguard for a pool party or event?

Yes. Dina provides NLS-certified lifeguarding for private pools, parties, and events — including an on-site safety brief and active supervision throughout.

What certifications does Dina hold?

Dina holds National Lifeguard (NLS), CPR-C, and Standard First Aid certifications, and has taught swimming professionally since 2019.
