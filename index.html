About Dina Farshchi

Dina Farshchi is the founder of Farsh Aquatics, a certified lifeguard and experienced swim coach. She has been teaching swimming since 2019 and served as a swim instructor and shift supervisor at a private club. Dina focuses on building strong relationships with her clients by offering personalized lessons and addressing individual needs. She is certified in CPR, National Lifeguarding, and First Aid, ensuring the highest safety standards. Dina is fluent in English and Farsi, and conversant in Spanish, allowing her to connect with a diverse range of students.
