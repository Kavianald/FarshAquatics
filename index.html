Good questions

Before you book

Where do lessons happen?

Across Vancouver and the North Shore, including West Vancouver and North Vancouver. Sessions can run at your home, condo or club pool — or at a partner facility if you don't have access to one.

What ages and levels do you teach?

All of them. Toddlers and kids building water confidence, adults refining stroke technique, and swimmers training for distance or endurance.

Can I hire a lifeguard for a pool party?

Yes. Dina provides NLS-certified lifeguarding for private pools, parties and events, including an on-site safety brief and active supervision for the whole session.

What certifications does Dina hold?

National Lifeguard (NLS), CPR-C, Standard First Aid and First Aid Attendee — and she has taught swimming professionally since 2019.

How much do lessons cost?

Rates depend on session type, location and how often you swim. Call or text (778) 345-7712, or email Dinafarshh@gmail.com, and Dina will send current pricing and availability.
